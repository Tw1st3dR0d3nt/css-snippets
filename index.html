INSTALL

Install a snippet

Choose your snippet

Browse the library and click Copy on the snippet you want to install.

IMPORT ORDER

Paste the snippet's @import URL at the very top of your CSS, before any other CSS rules.

B

BetterDiscord

  1. Open BetterDiscord Settings → Custom CSS.
  2. Choose a snippet and click Copy.
  3. Paste the copied import into your CSS.
  4. Save/apply your changes.
V

Vencord

  1. Open Vencord Settings → Vencord.
  2. Under Quick Actions, click Edit QuickCSS.
  3. Choose a snippet and click Copy.
  4. Paste the copied import into QuickCSS.
  5. Save/apply your changes.
